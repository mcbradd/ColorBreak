Privacy

ColorBreak currently requires no account. Break calculations and simulations run in your browser.

Optional product analytics

If an analytics endpoint is configured, ColorBreak sends only allow-listed events such as break creation, time-to-first-result buckets, remaining-slot count, sharing, and calculation errors. It does not send bids, acquisition costs, card names, search text, share contents, or persistent user identifiers. Session replay and sensitive-field capture are not used.

External links

Card details may include a clearly disclosed affiliate link when configured. Following it is optional; affiliate presence never changes analysis or ranking. External services apply their own privacy policies.

Future accounts

Saved scenarios, synchronization, and billing will receive an updated privacy notice before they launch. The free analytical experience will remain available if a paid entitlement fails.
