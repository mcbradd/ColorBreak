Methodology

ColorBreak estimates Magic: The Gathering break outcomes. It is a decision aid, not a prediction of the next opening or a guarantee of value.

Sources and evidence

MTGJSON supplies versioned product identities, contents, and weighted booster sheets. Scryfall supplies exact-printing metadata, images, and published timestamped price observations. Every sealed product is matched to its own constituent boosters and fixed cards. Official published facts take priority, then versioned community collation, then disclosed inference. Each fact has its own source; a verified card count does not certify every card's odds. Evidence for product identity, contents, collation, finish, break rules, and price freshness is tracked separately.

ColorBreak always gives its best available answer and improves it as better data arrives. Current exact observations take precedence over matching cached values, followed by a disclosed approximation. Stale or incomplete data never removes a bid estimate. Tap the circled asterisk beside a value or graphic for a short explanation of what could change it.

Expected value and outcomes

Analytic EV sums each possible printing's price times expected copies. Outcome distributions use deterministic, seeded sampling of mutually exclusive variants, sheet choices, fixed cards, nested products, finishes, and duplicate rules. MIN and MAX are calculated directly from possible pack choices, positive-weight variants, guaranteed cards, quantities, color constraints and duplicate rules. They include rare outcomes even when sampling never draws them. For a random spot, MIN and MAX span the remaining eligible slots. The quick preview already shows these bounds while its typical value is refined. Missing information or approximate pack rules can change the limits. Sampled results use 10,000 openings and can refine to 50,000. The same break state and price snapshot reproduce the same result. Weighted sequential sampling reserves space for known color requirements and treats alternate printings of one card as the same identity within a sheet. Unpublished factory order and box print-run correlations remain estimates.

The Outcome Fingerprint emphasizes median and downside alongside mean EV. A 20-dot display represents modeled frequency in five-percent increments. “Chance to clear” compares modeled counted card value with the entered landed cost.

Ignore bulk under

The bulk threshold excludes individual card finishes priced below the chosen amount from every counted-value calculation and visualization. It is a value filter, not a claim about liquidity, condition, fees, or the ability to sell a card.

Missing information

Missing information qualifies the answer instead of hiding it. Known pack slots, replacement rules, card weights and fixed contents survive incomplete data. Only when no structured component remains does a disclosed fallback sample cards independently; real packs may vary differently. Generic pack recipes are estimates, not confirmed product contents. A priced card with community or inferred odds remains in expected value and MAX, with an explanation of that uncertainty. An unpriced card contributes $0 to the known subtotal, which does not mean it is worthless. Prices do not silently cross printings or foil classes.

Seller estimates and actual results

Planning uses your acquisition costs first, available market prices next, and a disclosed $0 for unknown costs. Missing costs can understate break-even and overstate profit. Recorded-so-far results use recorded revenue and the best cost assumptions; they become actual profit only when receipts, costs and shipments reconcile. Target asks never become recorded sales.

Compliance

Seller scenarios have a dated platform-policy state. Prohibited Whatnot mechanics cannot generate show notes. Approval-required mechanics cannot export without a recorded approval reference. Policy classifications are informational and should be rechecked before each show.
