## Product statement

A price board for MTG Whatnot color breaks. Mobile-first (one-handed, portrait), with
mouse/keyboard as a working secondary target. Primary question, answered fast: *does this
slot have an edge at this price?* Buyers and sellers get separate views of the same
composition — a break of any mix of boxes/packs/bundles across sets — priced from live
Scryfall + tcgcsv market data.

**Entry flow (owner ruling, 2026-08-10):** set → product → quantity are the first three
controls on the page, numbered, in that order, and stay first — the builder is never
demoted below the board, because a returning user landing on a cached board could not find
where to change the set. The break itself collapses to one line (`#breakLine`) so the
Break Bar, slot EVs and the totals strip still clear gate G2 on the first screen; picking
a set keeps its bottom sheet, since that is the one step needing search and filters.
Covered by `test/e2e/s10-addbar.mjs`.

Four panel proposals were killed with reasons recorded in the review doc (cash-out
haircut, probability-of-profit, collector pool narrowing, ask-floor retightening) — do not
revive them without reading why they lost.

## Delegated decisions — how they resolved

The feedback doc explicitly left these to the implementer:

- **Mode-switch mechanism:** `body[data-view="buyer"|"seller"]` toggle in the header,
  driving `.buyer-only`/`.seller-only` visibility via CSS. Default view is buyer.
- **"Unreliable sealed price" heuristic:** absence of a sealed listing from tcgcsv is the
  trigger (tcgcsv exposes no sales-volume signal to threshold against) — see
  `chooseBasis` and its banner copy in `index.html`.
- **Orders model (surfaced mid-implementation, not in the original feedback doc):** an
  order is a buyer-chosen group of one or more won slots, not fixed one-order-per-slot —
  fulfillment cost and buyer S&H are editable per order. See
  `docs/adr/0001-orders-are-buyer-grouped-not-per-slot.md`.

Top-contributor count (10) was an owner ruling, not delegated — kept fixed.

## Out of scope (still binding)

- No change to price sources (Scryfall, tcgcsv.com) or the CORS relay approach.
- No build tooling — stays a single static `index.html`.
